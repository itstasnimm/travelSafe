**A custom-made application to make everyday travelling SAFE, without any hassle.**

---

## 🚀 Overview

travelSafe helps you stay safe while travelling by automatically tracking your journey and providing real-time updates to your trusted contacts, WITHOUT YOU needing to update!  

- Add your **current latitude & longitude**, **destination latitude & longitude**, and **checkpoints**. DONE!  
- No need to constantly update your progress — just share the **tracking link** generated by travelSafe.  
- travelSafe automatically keeps note of your **last known location** along with the **timestamp**.  
- Checkpoints are automatically **marked as "Reached"** based on your location.

---

## 🏥 Nearby Safety Features

Want to know about nearby hospitals or police stations?  
Just click the button and travelSafe will display the **top 2 nearest hospitals or police stations** on your route.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

- **Automatic Location Tracking** – Updates your progress in real-time.  
- **Checkpoint Tracking** – Marks checkpoints as reached based on your current location.  
- **Last Known Location** – Logs your location with timestamp.  
- **Nearby Safety Services** – Get nearby hospitals or police stations.  

---

## 💻 Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Java Spring Boot  
- **Frontend:** Thymeleaf, HTML, CSS, JavaScript  
- **Database:** PostgreSQL  
- **Deployment:** Render (Cloud)  
- **API:** Overpass API (for nearby safety services)  

---

## ⚡ How It Works

1. Enter your **current location**, **destination**, and **checkpoints**.  
2. Share the **tracking link** with your trusted contacts.  
3. travelSafe automatically updates your progress and checkpoints.  
4. Click the **Nearby Safety** button to see hospitals and police stations nearby.

---

## 🌐 Deployment

- Hosted on **Render** cloud platform.  
- PostgreSQL database used for storing user and journey data.
